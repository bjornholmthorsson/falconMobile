Admins can now manage and delete lunch menus, not just add them.

- Tap "Manage existing menus" on the Add lunch menu screen to see every saved week.
- Delete a menu that isn't locked to free up that week so you can re-add it.
- If people have already ordered, you'll see exactly who before anything is removed — confirm to delete the menu and their orders together.
- Locked weeks stay protected and can't be deleted.
