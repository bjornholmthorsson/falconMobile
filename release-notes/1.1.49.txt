Fixes a stuck home-screen badge counter, and adds an admin tool for lunch menus.

- The app-icon badge now clears when you open or foreground the app, not only when you tap "Clear all notifications" in your profile.
- Notifications you've already seen no longer leave the badge stuck.
- Admins: add a weekly lunch menu by uploading a screenshot — tap + on the Lunch Orders screen to import and review it.
