Log a whole day of hours from your calendar.

- New "Log Day from Calendar" option on the + button in Time: it reads every calendar entry for the day, matches each to a Jira issue, and writes them all at once.
- Review before it writes: tick entries off, see the matched issue on each, get warned about overlaps.
- Entries with no matching keyword rule are skipped, never guessed - tap Amend to fill one in by hand.
- Better keyword matching; entries already on your timesheet are left alone.
